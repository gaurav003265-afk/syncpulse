# Synthetic Stand-In Protocol Corpus
**Status**: STAND-IN CORPUS (Clearly Labelled)
**Purpose**: Talent AP Mega Hackathon 2026 - Problem H5 (Offline Frontline Health-Worker Companion)

This corpus contains 6 simplified public health protocol documents covering:
1. Antenatal Care Schedule (`01_antenatal_care_schedule.md`)
2. Immunisation Schedule (`02_immunisation_schedule.md`)
3. Danger Signs in Pregnancy and Newborns (`03_danger_signs_pregnancy_newborn.md`)
4. Malnutrition Screening (`04_malnutrition_screening.md`)
5. Hypertension and Diabetes Follow-Up (`05_hypertension_diabetes_ncd.md`)
6. ORS and Fever Management (`06_ors_fever_management.md`)

All clinical thresholds, dosages, danger signs, and schedules are strictly derived from official public health guidelines:
- Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India (ASHA/ANM modules, UIP, INAP, RBSK, NPCDCS).
- WHO Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI).

If organizer-provided official corpus files are supplied later at the finale, they will be placed in `data/corpus/` and ingested using the same pipeline.
